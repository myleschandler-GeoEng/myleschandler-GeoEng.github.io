# Myles Chandler MSci GMICE
Offshore cable engineering, GIS, and Python automation consultant.

I am a marine technologist and geotechnical consultant specialising in Cable Burial Risk Assessments (CBRA), Burial Assessment Studies, and offshore cable-protection engineering. I combine geotechnical, geophysical, Autonomous Identification System (AIS) and seabed-process evidence to develop route-specific burial, protection, and installation recommendations. I have worked on more than 2,000km of unique cable routes, providing technical leadership and driving innovation in probabilistic risk assessments, ground modelling, sediment mobility, scour, and secondary protection.

I am a principal contributor to the redevelopment of Intertek Metoc’s CBRA and Vertical Reference Level (VRL) workflow, I developed a daisy-chained Python and GIS process that reduces typical ground model preparation and probabilistic calculation from over 10-15 days, to approximately three days. Used by at least five colleagues, the process supports repeated project revisions while providing controlled engineering inputs, quality checks, traceability, and defined points for engineering review. The methods are sufficiently documented and scripts reviewed through an engineering governance procedure I developed as lead within a Coding Working Group at the company.

## Areas of expertise

- Ground modelling
- Sediment mobility and Vertical/Non-mobile Reference Levels
- Scour around offshore structures
- Python and GIS automation
